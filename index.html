Why clients stay

A plan is only worth the numbers underneath it

Anyone can produce forty pages of ambition. What a credit committee wants is a model whose assumptions are traceable, a cash-flow that still works when sales land 20% short, and an owner who can answer questions about both.

  • Assumption-driven models. Every figure links back to a driver you set, not a hard-coded number buried in a cell.
  • Downside first. Base, conservative and stress cases are built in before the plan leaves our desk.
  • Written in your voice. You present the plan; it has to read like your company, not a template.
  • Handover included. You keep the model, the sources and a walkthrough so you can update it yourself next year.
