Common questions

Before you send the form

Is the first call really free?

Yes, thirty minutes, no obligation and no invoice. If we cannot help, we will say so and point you somewhere more useful.

How quickly can you start?

Usually within one to two weeks. If your deadline is tighter, say so in the form — priority delivery is possible for a surcharge when capacity allows.

We are very early. Is it too soon to talk?

No. The cheapest time to fix a funding structure is before anything has been committed. Early conversations are often the shortest and the most useful.

What happens to the information we send?

It is used only to respond to your enquiry, stored in EU-hosted systems, and deleted on request. We sign an NDA before any sensitive material changes hands.
