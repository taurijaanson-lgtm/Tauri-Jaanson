About

Small practice, senior work, no handover to a junior

Jaanson Advisory is a boutique financial advisory practice. The person you meet on the first call is the person who builds your model and sits with you in front of the bank.

The practice

Why this exists

Good companies get turned down for bad reasons: a budget in the wrong structure, a forecast nobody can trace, an application that missed a formal requirement on page four of the guidelines. The underlying business was fine — the paperwork was not.

The large advisory firms price that work out of reach for a 30-person manufacturer. The cheap end produces templates that a credit committee recognises on sight. This practice sits in the middle: senior-level work, fixed fees, and a document your own team can defend after we leave.

We work with founders, owner-managed companies and public-sector project owners across Estonia, the Baltics and the wider EU, in English or Estonian.

Principles

Six rules we do not bend

Say no early

If a project is not fundable, you hear it on the first call — not after you have paid for a plan that goes nowhere.

Fixed fees

The price is agreed in writing before work starts. Hourly billing rewards slow work; we would rather be judged on the result.

Every number traceable

If you cannot follow a figure back to an assumption you set, it does not belong in the model.

Downside before upside

The stress case is built first. A plan that only works in the base case is a marketing document.

You own the files

Unlocked model, editable plan, documented sources. Next year's update should not require hiring us again.

No guaranteed approvals

Nobody can promise a funder's decision. We promise an application that cannot be rejected on quality.

Engagement model

How we work together

  • One point of contact. No account manager layer, no rotating team.
  • Written scope. Deliverables, assumptions, timeline and fee on one page.
  • Weekly check-in. Fifteen minutes, so nothing drifts for a fortnight unnoticed.
  • Confidentiality by default. NDA signed before the first document changes hands.
  • Available after delivery. Questions from your lender are answered, not invoiced.
Independence

We are not selling you a product

We take no commission from banks, leasing companies, funds or software vendors. Our only revenue is the fee you pay, which means the funding structure we recommend is the one we believe is right for the project — including when the answer is “borrow less” or “wait for the next application round”.

Where a specialist is genuinely needed — tax structuring, legal drafting, technical engineering — we say so and work alongside them rather than pretending the expertise is in-house.
