FAQ

Fees, scope and the awkward questions

How do payments work?

Half on signature, half on delivery of the final version. For engagements over €5,000 we split it into three milestone invoices. Payment terms are 14 days.

What if the scope changes halfway through?

Small changes are absorbed. If something material shifts — a different funding instrument, a second company in the group, an added market — we quote the extra work before doing it. You will never see an invoice you did not approve.

Can you work with the plan we already have?

Yes. A review-and-rebuild is usually 30–40% cheaper than starting from scratch. Send what you have and we will tell you honestly whether it is worth keeping.

How much of our time does this take?

Expect a 90-minute kickoff, a checklist of documents in week one, and two review rounds of roughly two hours each. Beyond that we work from the material you send.

Who owns the result?

You do, in full, once the final invoice is paid. We keep a copy for our records under the confidentiality terms and use nothing publicly without your written consent.

What if the bank rejects it?

We go through the rejection with you at no charge, and if the reason is something within the document’s control, we fix it and resubmit within the original fee.

Know the price before you commit

Send us the project and you get a written fixed-fee proposal within two working days — deliverables, timeline and what we need from you, on one page.
